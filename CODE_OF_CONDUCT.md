# Code of Conduct

This learning community welcomes people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Ask questions in good faith, explain disagreements with evidence, and critique ideas without attacking people.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, and publication of private information are not acceptable. Respect others’ time and avoid spam or repeated off-topic promotion.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact. Reports involving private information should use GitHub’s reporting tools rather than public issue comments.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces representing this project.
